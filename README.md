## ⚡ About Me

I'm a Computer Science (AI & ML) builder focused on the intersection of **local AI pipelines**, **automated media tooling**, and **Minecraft client modification**. I like taking complex workflows—whether it's rendering block schematics in-game or hunting down highlight moments in multi-hour VODs—and turning them into fast, local-first software that runs cleanly on consumer hardware.
